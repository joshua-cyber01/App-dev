# My Favorite Series: Breaking Bad

> "I am the one who knocks."

## About
**Breaking Bad** is a *crime drama* series about Walter White, a high school chemistry teacher who turns to making illegal drugs after he is diagnosed with cancer. The story follows how his choices change his life and the people around him.

---

## Main Characters
- Walter White
- Jesse Pinkman
- Skyler White
- Hank Schrader
- Saul Goodman

## Why I Like It
1. Intense and exciting story
2. Amazing acting
3. Characters that change over time
